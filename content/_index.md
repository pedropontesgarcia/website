I am a graduate student in [Computer Science at Cornell University](https://bowers.cornell.edu/) working toward a [Master of Science](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/master-science-computer-science). I am part of the [Capra research group](https://capra.cs.cornell.edu/) under Adrian Sampson, where I research problems in systems, like hardware design, through the lens of domain-specific languages and compilers. I previously researched abstraction problems and profiling for the [Calyx project](https://calyxir.org/), a compiler infrastructure for hardware accelerator generation. I am also a lead teaching assistant for [CS 3410](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs3410/2025fa/), Cornell's systems class. I have an interest in teaching and was nominated for for my college's annual TA award in 2025.

I am broadly interested in programming languages, compilers and systems. I enjoy topics with a theoretical flavor, such as language design and implementation, type systems, type theory, formal verification, and functional programming.

Outside of computer science, I teach for-credit outdoor courses with [Cornell Outdoor Education](https://scl.cornell.edu/coe), including cross-country skiing, backpacking, and canoe camping classes in the Adirondacks. I enjoy spending time in the outdoors, skiing, canoeing, and taking photographs. I am also interested in comparative literature and media studies, and came close to finishing a [minor](https://complit.cornell.edu/) on it at Cornell.
