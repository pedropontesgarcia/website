The **Bril Development Suite (BDS)** is a collection of tools for optimizing and analyzing Bril programs. For usage information, see the [documentation](https://codeberg.org/pedropontesgarcia/bril/src/branch/feat/bds/docs/README.md).

## Development

**BDS** is being developed by Helen Ge and Pedro Pontes García for the Cornell graduate compilers course, CS 6120.
